What the research means for EDGE

Value & visible outcomes

McKinsey's global June 2026 report describes more informed, value-conscious shoppers; credible lower-priced skincare; a split between premium and mass haircare; and renewed interest in bolder and hybrid makeup. These are global product trends, not a measured Gold Coast service forecast.

Discovery → proof → booking

EDGE interpretation: use Instagram for own-work examples, local search for intent, and reviews, service details and prices for confidence. Preserve one clear booking path and record where each lead came from.

Local signal, limited sample

GLOW's Q2 2026 survey of 2,840 Australian women aged 22–55 reports 34% reducing heritage-prestige spend. Its email-list sample is not nationally representative; use this as a prompt to test value messaging, not as a population estimate.

Launch zones & partner map

Planning clusters, not verified demand rankings. Start with travel radius and artist availability; compare local enquiries before allocating budget.

Central coast

Southport, Surfers Paradise, Broadbeach, Bundall and Main Beach. Test event, hotel and creator partnerships, alongside local maintenance services.

Southern coast

Miami, Burleigh, Palm Beach, Currumbin, Tugun and Coolangatta. Test local repeat services and event referrals within manageable travel times.

Northern & inland

Robina, Varsity Lakes, Nerang, Helensvale, Hope Island, Coomera and Pimpama. Test convenient appointments and neighbourhood partner referrals.

Partner types: wedding venues, photographers, planners, hairdressers, nail technicians, qualified skin practitioners, hotels and local studios. Track referral source, customer permission, service scope and booking outcome.

Adjust the assumptions to test a service business. This is an illustration, not a forecast, population estimate or total addressable market. Revenue is before costs, taxes and any artist/platform split.
